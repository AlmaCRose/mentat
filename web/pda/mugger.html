Mentat Mugger

Work In Progress

A button over your Torn pages that opens Mugger: while you're flying out or abroad, the players in (or flying to) that country who trade costly abroad items, with their estimated cash on hand, what a mug would take, and the fight. Works in Torn PDA, desktop browsers and Safari on iPad/iPhone. For members of the faction's Mentat server.

What it does

  • Who trades: your Mentat server keeps a 90-day market database of bazaar listings of abroad items (from Weav3r, every 30 minutes): who sells them, how much, and when they restock or sell. Torn's own bazaar directories add the busiest bazaars for each abroad item and their weekly customers (every 12 hours, with the server's key). A restock of thousands of items counts as several trips, not one, and only more than 5 of an item counts as trading: someone selling one or two isn't assumed to be doing trips (smaller listings and restocks are still shown, marked as not counted).
  • Only abroad: players in Torn can bank their money, so Mugger only looks at players abroad. It starts once you fly out: the best traders for your destination are looked up on Torn with your own API key to see who is there or flying there.
  • Cash on hand (estimate): their trip money while they're still flying out, plus bazaar and item market sales since they left Torn (that money goes to their wallet). Their bazaars are re-read every 5 minutes and their item market revenue every 5 minutes (you can make it 2 or 10) while they're in your country. What they had before isn't known.
  • Mug take: from the Torn wiki: 5% of cash on hand, rolled up to 10% (low rolls far likelier), × (1 + Masterful Looting + Plunder). Your Masterful Looting is read from your merits; set your weapon's Plunder in Settings. Players at a 7★ Clothing Store are flagged (mugs take 75% less), and so is anyone seen in hospital in your trip's country in the last 12 hours: mugged when the hospital reason says so, otherwise may be mug-protected (a mug protects what is left in their wallet, decaying over 12 hours). Torn only shows a hospital stay while it lasts, so players who were out before you started watching are caught by their "fights lost abroad" count instead (lost N abroad): Torn keeps it by day and doesn't say which country.
  • Two scores: one for the country you're in (only items sold there count, plus whether they sell there or travel there), and one overall (every abroad item). Sort by either. Two thirds of the traders checked on a trip are the best for that country; the rest are the best overall, in case a big trader happens to be there.
  • The fight: your battle stats against FFScouter's estimate of theirs, and fair fight, with your own FFScouter key (optional), once per player per trip.
  • Who's actually there (paste the People list): once you've landed, open Torn's People list for the country, select it and copy it, then paste it into the box in Mugger (a page at a time if there are several) and press Add to the list. From then on Mugger checks only those people, traders or not, and drops anyone who flies out. Copied with the links, every player comes with their ID; as plain text, names are matched against the market database first and otherwise looked up with Torn's player search (one call per name, with your key). Mugger never reads Torn's pages itself: you copy the list. Clear list goes back to the best traders, and a new trip starts without a list.
  • In Torn: no API calls; Mugger shows a preview of a country's traders from the database.

Add it to Torn PDA

  1. Make sure your API key is set in Torn PDA (a Limited key is best).
  2. Open Settings → Advanced browser settings and turn on Enable custom user scripts.
  3. Tap Manage scripts, press + to add a script, and tap Configure.
  4. Paste this link into Remote URL:
  5. Fetch the script, check its name is Mentat Mugger, and save it. Open any Torn page: it signs you in with Torn PDA's key.

Add it to a desktop browser (Tampermonkey, Violentmonkey, Greasemonkey)

  1. Install Tampermonkey (or Violentmonkey; Greasemonkey on Firefox).
  2. Chrome and Edge only: open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions), click Details under Tampermonkey and turn on Allow User Scripts.
  3. Open Tampermonkey's Dashboard, go to Utilities, and under Install from URL paste this address and click Install:
    In Greasemonkey or Violentmonkey, opening the script address shows the install screen.
  4. Click Install. It keeps itself up to date from this server.
  5. Open a Torn page, tap the Mugger button (the money bag) and sign in with your Torn API key.

    Then open the Userscripts extension from Safari's address bar and tap Install.

FFScouter (optional)

Sign up at ffscouter.com with your Torn API key (free). Then in Mugger: ⚙ → FFScouter, paste the key (in Torn PDA you can leave it empty to use PDA's key), agree to FFScouter's data policy and tap Turn on FFScouter. It's used once per player per trip; requests go only to ffscouter.com.

Your key and your data

To check you're a member, your key is sent once to the Mentat server, which asks Torn who you are and forgets it. Everything else Mugger looks up on Torn uses your own key, from your device, and only while you're flying out or abroad (plus your own status once a minute, to know when you fly). Nothing you look up is sent to the Mentat server. In a browser the key is kept in the script's own storage on your device and only sent to api.torn.com; signing out removes it. Players in your country are checked again every minute (you can make it 2 or 3), players flying there every 3 minutes and the rest every 20. You choose how many calls a minute Mugger may use (20 to start with; Torn allows 100 a minute across all your tools).
