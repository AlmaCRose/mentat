Mentat bubbles for Torn PDA

Small floating bubbles over your Torn pages with live countdowns, the same ones Mentat shows on the desktop. For members of the faction's Mentat server.

  • LandingCountdown while you fly, "abroad" while you're away.
  • ChainThe timer while a chain runs: amber under 2 minutes, red under 1.
  • Energy and nerveCurrent value, green when full. Life is optional.
  • CooldownsDrug, then booster, then medical, while one is running.
  • Ranked warCountdown to the start, then your lead.

Bubbles only show when there's something to show. Tap one to open its Torn page, drag it anywhere, and hold it for settings (turn bubbles on or off, reset their places, sign out).

The Mentat button at the top of the stack hides or shows every bubble with one tap. While they're hidden it shows the most urgent countdown (the chain timer, else your landing time). Drag it and the bubbles follow; you can turn it off in the settings.

Add it to Torn PDA

  1. Make sure your API key is set in Torn PDA (a Limited key).
  2. Open Torn PDA's Settings → Advanced browser settings and turn on Enable custom user scripts.
  3. Tap Manage scripts, then + to add a script.
  4. Choose the Remote load/update tab and paste this address:
  5. Fetch the script, check its name is Mentat bubbles, and save it.
  6. Open any Torn page in Torn PDA. The first time, the bubbles check you're in the faction (a few seconds); after that they appear straight away.

Torn PDA keeps the script up to date from that address, and tells you when there's a new version if you turned on its script update notices.

Your key and your data

The script uses the API key you already gave Torn PDA (Torn PDA fills it in; you don't type it here).

To check you're a member, the key is sent once to the Mentat server, which asks Torn who you are and which faction you're in, then forgets the key. It isn't stored or logged there. The sign-in it gets back is kept in Torn PDA's own script storage.

The bubbles get your bars, cooldowns, travel and chain from Torn's API with your key once a minute, and your faction's ranked war every 5 minutes. Nothing goes anywhere else.

They only display: the script never reads, changes or clicks anything on Torn's pages, and tapping a bubble just opens the matching page.

If something's wrong

No bubbles? They only show when there's something to show; hold any visible bubble for the settings. Check Enable custom user scripts is on and the script is switched on in Manage scripts.

"Mentat is for members of …" Your key belongs to someone outside the faction. If you should have access, ask an officer to add you to Mentat's allow-list. The bubbles try again after 12 hours, or remove and re-add the script.

"Check the API key in Torn PDA" Torn rejected the key. Set a working key in Torn PDA's settings and reload a page.
