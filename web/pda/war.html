Mentat war board

A button over your Torn pages that opens the war board: the enemy faction with dibs, and the faction's hit queue with the time each person hits. Works in Torn PDA and in desktop browsers. For members of the faction's Mentat server.

What it does

  • Enemy: your war opponent's members with status, hospital and jail countdowns, landing estimates and last action, sorted and filtered like Mentat's War tab. Dibs (the same claims as Mentat's War tab) show who's going for whom, in order. Live (3, 5 or 10 s) makes the enemy refresh faster during a war while you have the list open.
  • Hit queue: join with or without a target (anyone, in the enemy faction or not). Everyone hits when the chain timer shows the hit speed (2:00 to start with), so each person gets an estimated time, in countdown and TCT. Times follow the real timer, so an early or late hit moves everyone after it, and the board shows how far each time has moved. Your hit comes off the queue by itself once Torn's chain report shows it.
  • Hit speed: set by chain leads: the server owner, server admins and the faction positions the owner ticks in the board's settings.
  • Attack pages: a red light, Warning: Not your turn, while someone else is next in the queue; amber when you're next but it's early; green when it's your turn.
  • Bonus hits: during a war, when the next hit is a chain bonus (10, 25, 50, 100, 250 …) and the player you're about to attack isn't in the faction you're at war with: BONUS HIT: DO NOT HIT OUTSIDE.
  • FFScouter (optional): fair fight and estimated battle stats with your own FFScouter key (how to add it). The FFScouter tab searches FFScouter's target lists by level, fair fight and activity (or its best respect and level hunting lists), with + Queue and Attack on each result.

Add your FFScouter key (optional)

FFScouter adds fair fight scores and estimated battle stats to the enemy list and queued targets, and the Around your range filter and the FFScouter tab's target search need it. The steps are the same in Torn PDA, desktop browsers and Safari. Do them once on each device you use the board on, because the key is kept on that device.

  1. Sign up at ffscouter.com with your Torn API key (it's free). Once it's registered there, that Torn API key is your FFScouter key.
  2. Open any Torn page and tap the war board button.
  3. Tap ⚙ at the top of the board to open Settings, and find the FFScouter section.
  4. Paste your key into the FFScouter key box. In Torn PDA you can leave it empty to use Torn PDA's own key, if that's the key you registered.
  5. Read FFScouter's data policy and tick the box to agree to it.
  6. Tap Turn on FFScouter. Settings then shows it as on, with your account type (free or premium).

If the board says the key isn't registered, sign up at ffscouter.com with that key first. Your own key is needed because fair fight is worked out against your stats. Requests go only to ffscouter.com and don't count against your Torn API limit. FFScouter's premium gives more accurate estimates. To stop, tap Turn off in the same place.

Your key and your data

To check you're a member, your Torn API key is sent once to the Mentat server, which asks Torn who you are and forgets it. In a browser the script keeps the key on your device only (its own storage), to read your own data from Torn for Live, energy sharing and your stats; it's never sent anywhere else, and signing out removes it. A Limited key is recommended; energy sharing needs at least Minimal.

The board makes no Torn API calls with your key: the Mentat server reads the chain, the enemy and who hit with its own key, and everyone's board asks it every few seconds while open.

FFScouter is off until you turn it on in the board's settings. It then sends the board's player IDs and your FFScouter key to ffscouter.com only; their data policy applies.

The script only shows and links: it never reads, changes or clicks anything on Torn's pages. The attack-page warnings look only at the page address.

Energy sharing (opt-in)

If you turn it on (the board asks once, and it's in Settings), your board reads your energy and drug cooldown from Torn with your own key about once a minute while you have Torn open, and sends them to your faction's Mentat server. The Faction tab then shows them on your row, and adds your energy (and hits, at 25 energy each) to the "~Online E available" total while you're online or idle. Turning it off deletes your readings at once.
